Hardware Architecture

Engineered to Disappear.
Built to Protect.

BronchFlow rests seamlessly on the sternum. Featuring medical-grade hypoallergenic silicone, biocompatible sensors, and a featherlight chassis designed for 24/7 day and night tracking.

Medical Protocol

Four Steps from Sensor to Specialist.

An effortless closed-loop framework engineered to operate unobtrusively in the background.

PHASE 01

Wear & Calibrate

Place the sensor on the sternum. BronchFlow establishes an individualized baseline of your thoracic motion and breath sounds.

PHASE 02

Continuous Sensing

Acoustic and motion sensors continuously monitor respiration, tracking airway turbulence, wheezes, and night coughing.

PHASE 03

Pre-Attack Alerts

When early airway narrowing is identified, an immediate notification is sent with personalized remediation guidance.

PHASE 04

Clinical Synchronization

Your pulmonologist receives longitudinal objective data, eliminating recall bias during clinical consultations.

Edge Neural Inference
